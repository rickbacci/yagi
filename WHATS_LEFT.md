# What's left

The pause buffer is the open piece.

Space pauses the picture. Tuner 0 keeps writing `live.ts`. The playhead stays where you paused, and the file keeps growing for as long as TV stays open. Close TV deletes it. It is not a recording, and the library cap does not apply to it. Nothing yet decides how long that pause is allowed to run, or what happens to the video that piled up while you were behind live.

Skip is 10 seconds, 5 near live. The picture overlay and the flyout are settled. A pass through the app is what will show if anything else is still wrong.
